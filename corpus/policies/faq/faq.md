# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

**Can I change the delivery address after ordering?**
Only within one hour of placing the order, and only if it has not yet been
dispatched. Contact support with your order number as soon as possible — the
address cannot be changed from the account page once the order is confirmed. If
the parcel has already been dispatched, we cannot redirect it.

**Can I cancel an order?**
Yes, if it has not been dispatched. After dispatch, wait for delivery and then
start a return.

**I ordered the wrong colour. What now?**
Return the item as a change of mind and place a new order. We cannot swap the
variant on an existing order.

## Returns and refunds

**How long do I have to send something back?**
Thirty days from when the parcel arrives — not from when you ordered it. If you
ordered on the 1st and it arrived on the 5th, your last day is the 4th of the
following month.

**How long do I have after getting my RMA code?**
Roughly two weeks. Send the parcel within that time or the code expires and you
will need to request a new one.

**Do I pay for return shipping?**
Not if the box is still sealed — we send a prepaid label. If you have opened the
item, €4.90 comes off the refund to cover the return.

**When do I get my money back?**
Five to seven business days after the item arrives back with us and passes
inspection. Your bank may take another day or two to show it.

**Can I return earbuds?**
Not once the hygiene seal is broken. This applies to the Pulse Earbuds and to any
other in-ear product. If they are faulty, that is a warranty matter and is
handled differently.

## Shipping

**When is shipping free?**
Standard shipping is free on orders over €50.

**What time is the cut-off?**
14:00 CET on business days. Orders after that go out the next business day.

**Why is my tracking link empty?**
Tracking can take up to 12 hours to update after the parcel is handed over. If
there is still nothing after a day, contact support.

## Products

**What is the difference between the Aurora and the Aurora Pro?**
The Pro lasts 45 hours instead of 30, cancels more noise (35 dB against 25 dB),
supports LDAC, and pauses when you take it off. Both are over-ear and use USB-C.

**Do the headphones work with a cable?**
Yes. Both Aurora models include a 3.5 mm cable and work passively when the
battery is flat, though noise cancellation is off in that mode.

**Which products are waterproof?**
The Nimbus speaker is IPX7 and can be submerged briefly. The Pulse Earbuds are
IPX4, which covers sweat and light rain but not submersion. Nothing else in the
range is water resistant.

## Warranty

**How long is the guarantee?**
Twenty-four months, as required by EU law. Batteries and charging cases are
covered for twelve months.

**My battery does not last as long as it used to. Is that covered?**
If capacity falls below 70% of the rated figure within the first twelve months,
yes. Gradual decline after that is normal wear.

**Does opening the case void the guarantee?**
Yes. Opening the housing or having it repaired by a third party ends the
guarantee on that unit.
