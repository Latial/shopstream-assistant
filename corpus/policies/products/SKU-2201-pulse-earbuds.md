# Pulse Earbuds (SKU-2201)

Compact true wireless in-ear earbuds with a pocketable charging case.

**Price: €89**

## Specifications

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | In-ear, true wireless |
| Battery life | 7 hours per charge, 21 additional hours from the case |
| Charging | USB-C and Qi wireless |
| Water resistance | IPX4 (sweat and light rain) |
| Bluetooth | 5.3, SBC and AAC |
| Weight | 4.6 g per earbud, 42 g case |
| Colours | Black, White |

## In the box

Earbuds, charging case, USB-C cable (0.5 m), three sizes of silicone tips.

## Hygiene seal and returns

The retail box carries a hygiene seal. Once that seal is broken these earbuds
cannot be returned as a change of mind, in line with the exclusions in the
Returns Policy. Faulty units are covered by the warranty as normal.

## Known issues

Units manufactured before March 2026 could show a case-charging fault where the
right earbud does not seat correctly. Affected units are replaced under warranty.
