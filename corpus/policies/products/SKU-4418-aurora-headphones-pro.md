# Aurora Headphones Pro (SKU-4418)

The Pro version of the Aurora over-ear headphones, with a larger battery, deeper
noise cancellation and high-resolution codec support.

**Price: €229**

## Specifications

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Over-ear, closed back |
| Battery life | 45 hours (ANC on) |
| Charging | USB-C, 2 hours to full, 10-minute quick charge for 6 hours |
| Noise cancellation | Adaptive hybrid ANC, up to 35 dB |
| Bluetooth | 5.3, SBC, AAC and LDAC |
| Weight | 265 g |
| Colours | Black, Sand, Navy |

## What the Pro adds over the standard Aurora

- 45 hours of battery life instead of 30.
- Adaptive noise cancellation reaching 35 dB rather than a fixed 25 dB.
- LDAC support for high-resolution streaming.
- Wear detection: playback pauses when you take the headphones off.

## In the box

Headphones, USB-C charging cable (1.5 m), 3.5 mm audio cable, hard shell case,
airline adapter, quick start guide.

## Known issues

Wear detection can trigger while wearing a thick winter hat. Sensitivity can be
lowered, or the feature disabled, in the companion app.
