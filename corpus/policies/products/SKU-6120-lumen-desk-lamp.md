# Lumen Desk Lamp (SKU-6120)

Adjustable LED desk lamp with tuneable colour temperature.

**Price: €69**

## Specifications

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Colour temperature | 2700 K to 6500 K |
| Brightness | 800 lumens maximum, stepless dimming |
| Colour rendering | CRI 95 |
| Power | 12 W, USB-C powered |
| Reach | 45 cm arm |
| Colours | White, Graphite |

## In the box

Lamp, weighted base, desk clamp, USB-C power adapter (EU plug), USB-C cable
(1.8 m).

## Mounting

The lamp ships with both a weighted base and a clamp. The clamp fits desks up to
6 cm thick.

## Known issues

The lamp remembers the last brightness setting but not the last colour
temperature; it returns to 4000 K after being unplugged.
