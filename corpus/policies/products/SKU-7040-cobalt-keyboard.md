# Cobalt Mechanical Keyboard (SKU-7040)

75% layout hot-swappable mechanical keyboard.

**Price: €139**

## Specifications

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Layout | 75%, 82 keys |
| Switches | Hot-swappable, 3-pin and 5-pin, tactile brown fitted |
| Connection | USB-C wired, Bluetooth 5.1 (three devices), 2.4 GHz dongle |
| Battery life | 70 hours with backlight off, 20 hours with backlight on |
| Keycaps | PBT double-shot |
| Weight | 780 g |

## In the box

Keyboard, USB-C cable (1.8 m), 2.4 GHz dongle, keycap and switch puller, two
spare switches.

## Layouts

Shipped with an ISO layout. QWERTZ and AZERTY keycap sets are sold separately.
The firmware supports remapping through the browser-based configurator.

## Known issues

The 2.4 GHz dongle stores itself under the magnetic top plate; several customers
have reported not finding it and assuming it was missing.
