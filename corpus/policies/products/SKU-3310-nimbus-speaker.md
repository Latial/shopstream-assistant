# Nimbus Bluetooth Speaker (SKU-3310)

Portable waterproof speaker for outdoor use.

**Price: €119**

## Specifications

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery life | 18 hours at moderate volume |
| Charging | USB-C, 3 hours to full |
| Water resistance | IPX7 (submersible to 1 m for 30 minutes) |
| Bluetooth | 5.2, multipoint for two devices |
| Output | 30 W |
| Weight | 690 g |
| Colours | Charcoal, Forest |

## In the box

Speaker, USB-C cable (1 m), carry strap.

## Stereo pairing

Two Nimbus speakers can be paired for stereo playback. Pairing is done by holding
the Bluetooth button on both units for five seconds. Stereo pairing is not
supported with other models.
