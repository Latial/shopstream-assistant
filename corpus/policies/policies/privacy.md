# Privacy Policy

## What we store

We store the information needed to fulfil and support your orders: name,
delivery address, email address, order history and payment method type. We do not
store full card numbers; payments are processed by our payment provider.

## How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are retained for **7 years** to satisfy tax and
accounting obligations. Support conversations are retained for 24 months. Marketing
preferences are retained until you change them.

## Account deletion

You can request deletion of your account from the privacy settings page. We
complete deletion within **30 days** of the request. Records we are legally
required to keep, such as invoices, are retained for the statutory period in a
restricted archive and are not used for any other purpose.

## Marketing

Marketing email is opt-in and every message contains an unsubscribe link. Opting
out does not affect transactional messages such as order confirmations and
dispatch notices.

## Sharing

We share data only with the processors needed to run the service: our payment
provider, our carriers, and our email provider. We do not sell personal data.

## Contact

Data protection questions can be sent to the address listed on the contact page.
