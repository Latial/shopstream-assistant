# Warranty Policy

## What is covered

Every product sold carries the **24-month statutory conformity guarantee**
required under EU consumer law. This covers defects that were present at the time
of delivery, including faults that become apparent later.

Batteries, charging cases and included cables carry a **12-month manufacturer
warranty**. Battery capacity that has degraded to less than 70% of its rated
capacity within those 12 months is treated as a defect.

## What is not covered

The guarantee does not apply where the fault results from:

- Liquid damage on products that are not rated water resistant.
- Physical damage from dropping, crushing or misuse.
- The casing having been opened, or repair attempted by a third party.
- Normal cosmetic wear such as scratches on the housing or ear cushions.

## How a claim works

Contact support with your order number and a description of the fault. We may ask
for photographs or a short video. If the claim is accepted, we repair or replace
the item at our discretion. Where neither repair nor replacement is possible
within 30 days of the item reaching us, we refund the purchase price.

## Warranty and returns are different things

A return is a change of mind and must happen within 30 days of delivery. A
warranty claim concerns a defective product and can be made at any point in the
24-month period. You do not need to be inside the return window to make a
warranty claim, and warranty claims do not require an RMA code.

## Proof of purchase

The order in your account is sufficient proof of purchase. Items bought from
third-party resellers must be claimed through that reseller.
