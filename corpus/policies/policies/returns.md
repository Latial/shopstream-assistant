# Returns Policy

## Return window

You can return most items within **30 days of delivery**. The window runs from the
delivery date shown on your order page, not from the date you placed the order.
Requests submitted after day 30 are declined automatically by the returns system.

## Unopened items

If the manufacturer's seal is intact and the packaging is undamaged, we cover
return shipping. Request a return from your account and we email you a prepaid
label together with your RMA code.

## Opened items

Opened items can still be returned if they are complete and in resalable
condition, including all cables, adapters and documentation. For opened items a
return shipping fee of **€4.90** is deducted from your refund.

## Exclusions

The following cannot be returned:

- In-ear headphones and earbuds once the hygiene seal has been broken.
- Items marked **final sale** on the product page (clearance stock).
- Gift cards and downloadable software licences.

A faulty item is never treated as an exclusion. If the product is defective, see
the Warranty Policy instead — that covers 24 months, not 30 days.

## RMA process

1. Open the order in your account and choose "Return this item".
2. We issue an RMA code in the format `RMA-482913` within one business day.
3. Ship the item back within **14 days** of the RMA code being issued. Codes
   expire after 14 days and a new request must be made.
4. Include the RMA code on the outside of the parcel.

## Refund timing

Once the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection, the refund is
issued to the original payment method within **5–7 business days**. Bank
processing may add a further two days depending on your provider.
