# Shipping Policy

## Delivery options

| Option | Delivery time | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–5 business days | €4.90 |
| Express | 1–2 business days | €9.90 |
| Next-day | Next business day | €14.90 |

**Standard shipping is free on orders over €50.** The threshold is calculated on
the order value after discounts and before shipping costs.

## Order cut-off

Orders placed before **14:00 CET** on a business day are dispatched the same day.
Orders placed after the cut-off, at weekends, or on public holidays are dispatched
on the next business day. Next-day delivery is only available for orders that
clear the 14:00 cut-off.

## Delivery zones

**EU Zone 1** — Germany, France, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Italy, Spain.
Standard delivery 3–5 business days.

**EU Zone 2** — Poland, Czechia, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Greece, Portugal,
Sweden, Denmark, Finland. Standard delivery 5–7 business days.

Deliveries within the European Union include all duties and import VAT. For
deliveries outside the European Union, any customs duties and import taxes are
paid by the recipient on arrival and are not included in the price shown at
checkout.

## Tracking

A tracking link is emailed when the parcel is handed to the carrier. Tracking
information can take up to 12 hours to appear after dispatch.

## Delays

Between **20 November and 24 December** delivery estimates may extend by 1–2
business days because of seasonal volume. Weather and carrier disruptions are
outside our control; if a parcel is more than 10 business days late, contact
support and we will open a carrier investigation.
